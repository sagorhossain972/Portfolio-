"Determinants of Citizen Adoption of the EUDI Wallet for Health Services in Finland: A Systematic Review and Conceptual Framework" 1st Author

This paper investigates the adoption of the European Digital Identity Wallet (EUDI Wallet) within Finnish healthcare services. Utilizing a PRISMA 2020-guided systematic literature review methodology, the research synthesizes 74 relevant studies.

The key finding is the development of a comprehensive, three-layered conceptual framework that integrates UTAUT2 acceptance factors with Privacy Calculus Theory to explain and predict citizen adoption behaviors in secure digital health ecosystems.

02

"A Data-Driven Approach to Sustainable Agriculture" 1st Author

Authored in collaboration with Aspire to Innovate (a2i), this research explores the transition toward digital, data-centric agricultural models.

The methodology relies on a comparative assessment of global data-driven agricultural practices, specifically drawing on advanced technological case studies from India, Estonia, and Singapore. Based on these global insights, the paper proposes a tailored, localized smart agriculture framework designed to implement a sustainable and efficient data-driven ecosystem within Bangladesh.

03

"Bridging Digital Divides for Inclusive Healthcare in Bangladesh" 1st Author

This paper analyzes the structural shift in public health infrastructure by exploring the rapid transformation and emergence of digital health and telemedicine services, a shift primarily catalyzed by the COVID-19 pandemic.

The research focuses on the intersection of technological deployment and social equity, providing strategic frameworks and actionable insights aimed at building a resilient digital health future that ensures inclusive and equal healthcare access for all demographics.

04

"Causality and Dependence of COVID-19 Variables from Our World in Data" 2nd Author

This study provides a rigorous quantitative evaluation of early pandemic public health propositions.

The methodology employs advanced econometric modeling, specifically utilizing Pearson's Product Moment Correlation, Panel Granger Causality tests, and Multivariate Bayesian Vector Autoregression (VAR). By analyzing comprehensive datasets encompassing 209 countries, the research empirically establishes the causal relationships and dependencies between various COVID-19 outbreak variables to inform data-driven policy responses.
